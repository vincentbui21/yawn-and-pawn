# Epic 2 retrospective (short), 2026-10-06

**Scope:** 12 code stories (2.1–2.12) plus the device checklist (2.13), run unattended from 2026-10-05 21:20 to 2026-10-06 10:40 (about 13 h wall clock, including a 4.5 h stop on the Claude usage limit).

## What went well
- **Reviews paid off.** Two reviewers per story found real defects in every story (about 90 fixes in total), several of them alarm-safety bugs: the emergency backup slot not re-armed when storage failed, alarms not re-armed after a slow boot, a restore re-arming the old alarms, a ring that could stay silent after a call, volume keys blocked during a snooze.
- **Two lanes with stacked branches** kept both lanes busy; lanes picked the next story while the previous one was in review.
- **One PR per stack** (owner decision 2026-10-06) cut rebases and CI rounds; PR #28 merged five stories after one green CI.
- **Device checklist over adb** covered 14 of 20 items without the owner; reboot and lock-screen steps needed the owner for a few minutes only.

## What hurt
- **Claude usage limit** stopped all agents from 02:30 to 06:55; work resumed without loss, but four hours were lost.
- **GitHub CI runners:** "job was not acquired by Runner" twice and an emulator that hung while booting three times on one PR; main passed the same step around it.
- **The CI managed device is an ATD image** (no notification shade, few system apps): two instrumented tests (notification shade, Settings and dialer) had to be removed after they failed in CI.
- **Stacks built on unreviewed bases** needed extra conflict work when the review fixes landed (2.4 on 2.3, 2.7 on 2.11).
- **Test strength:** the 2.10 reviewers found scenario assertions that could not fail (billing checks with no billing adapter yet, shared fake inputs).

## Actions for Epic 3
1. Keep two lanes, two reviewers per story, high/medium fixes only, one PR per stack.
2. No androidTest that needs system apps, the shade or a camera; prefer Robolectric. QR scanning uses a fake camera source on the host; the real camera is a device check.
3. Start a lane's next story only on a reviewed-and-fixed base when the stories touch the same files; otherwise stack as before.
4. Keep agent concurrency at about four (two lanes plus their reviewers or fix agents) to stay under the usage limit.
5. Reviewers keep checking that each test assertion can actually fail.
6. Device ringing tests are batched into the Epic 3 checklist (3.14), with the test alarm at a low volume; owner-only items go to v2 (owner decision 2026-10-06).
