# Yawn & Pawn closed test: a guide for testers

Thank you for testing Yawn & Pawn. It is an Android alarm clock where every snooze costs money and waking up is free: you stop the alarm by finishing a short check, such as a few sums.

The test runs for 14 days, from **[START DATE: owner fills in]** to **[END DATE: owner fills in]**. Google only lets us publish the app if at least 12 testers stay in the test for 14 days in a row, so please stay in until the end date, even on days you don't use it much.

## Before anything else

**Keep a second alarm.** This is a test version. An alarm may ring late or not at all. Every morning, keep your usual alarm (another app or a clock) set a few minutes after the Yawn & Pawn alarm. Don't use Yawn & Pawn as your only alarm for anything important, like a flight or an exam.

**Snooze payments are free in this test, but only if we added you.** We add each tester's Google account to Google Play's test-payment list. Then every snooze is a test order and nothing is charged.

- When you snooze, Google Play's payment sheet opens. For a tester it shows a test payment method, such as "Test card, always approves".
- **If the sheet shows your own card or account balance and no test card, stop.** Press back, don't pay, and tell us. It means your account is not on the list yet.
- Google may send you a receipt email for a test order. It says it is a test, and you are not charged.

## 1. Join the test (5 minutes)

1. Open the opt-in link on your Android phone: **[OPT-IN LINK: owner fills in]**
2. Sign in with the **same Google account you gave us**, then tap "Become a tester".
3. Tap the link to Google Play on that page and install Yawn & Pawn from there. Don't install a file that someone sends you: payments only work when the app comes from Google Play.
4. If Google Play says the app isn't available, wait an hour and try again. If it still fails, tell us.

## 2. Set it up (10 minutes, the first day)

1. Open the app and follow the setup. It explains how the alarm behaves and asks you to choose a snooze fee (in this test, nothing is charged).
2. Create your first alarm.
3. On "Make sure it rings", go through every item and fix what it marks. This is the reliability checklist. It is also in **Settings > Make sure alarms ring**.
4. Ring the test alarm with your screen locked. It should light up the screen and ring over the lock screen. A test alarm never charges you.
5. The app asks if you want to share anonymous usage stats. Either answer is fine for the test.

## 3. Each morning

Use Yawn & Pawn for your real wake-up, with your second alarm set a few minutes later. Then mix these up over the 14 days:

- **Wake up for free:** tap "I'm up" and finish the check. The alarm goes quiet while you do it.
- **Snooze a few times:** tap Snooze, confirm, and pay with the test card. Check that the snooze starts and the alarm rings again later. Each snooze in the same morning should cost more than the last.
- **Try every check:** Math, Word Unscramble, Memory Sequence and QR/Barcode (scan a code you put in another room, such as a barcode on a box).
- **Once a week:** open **Settings > Make sure alarms ring** and fix anything it marks. Phone updates sometimes turn settings back off.
- **Once a week, a locked test alarm:** lock your phone, then ring a test alarm (from the alarm editor's ⋮ menu, "More options > Test alarm", or "Ring a test alarm" in the checklist). Check that it shows on the lock screen and rings. Test alarms are always free.

If you have time, these help us a lot too:

- Restart your phone at night with an alarm set for the morning.
- Take or make a phone call while the alarm rings.
- Press Home while the alarm rings, open another app, then come back through the notification.
- Turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data while the alarm rings. Snooze should say it is unavailable, and the check should still work.
- Look at the Progress tab after a few mornings.

**Please don't** force-stop the app, or close it with your phone's "clean up" or "close app" tools. That cancels its alarms until you open it again. Swiping it away in the recent apps list is fine.

## 4. The phones we need most

So far the app has been tested on one Oppo phone (ColorOS) with Android 13. Phone makers save battery in different ways, and that is what most often stops an alarm. We especially need:

- **Samsung** (One UI)
- **Xiaomi, Redmi or POCO** (HyperOS or MIUI)
- **Google Pixel**
- **OnePlus, Realme, Vivo, iQOO, Huawei, Honor, Motorola, Nokia or Sony**
- **Older Android versions** (8 to 11) and **the newest ones** (14, 15 and 16)

You can find your Android version in your phone's **Settings > About phone**. Please tell us your phone model and Android version in your first report, even if everything works.

## 5. Tell us what happened

- **Feedback form (best):** **[FEEDBACK FORM LINK: owner fills in]**. It takes about 2 minutes.
- **Email:** <support@yawnandpawn.com> (the address on our [support page](https://yawnandpawn.com/support/)).
- **Google Play:** you can also leave private feedback on the app's page in Google Play. Only we see it.

Please always tell us:

- your phone model and Android version;
- the date and time it happened (and the time the alarm should have rung);
- what you did, what you saw, and what you expected;
- a screenshot if you can.

Tell us right away if:

- an alarm didn't ring, rang late or stopped by itself;
- you couldn't stop the alarm, or your phone felt stuck;
- you were asked to pay with a real card, or charged real money;
- a snooze you paid for didn't start;
- the app crashed or froze;
- something was confusing.

Never send us your card details or passwords.

## 6. Your data

There is no account. Your alarms, history and payments stay on your phone. Crash reports are sent automatically so we can fix alarms that fail. Usage stats are sent only if you said yes, and you can change that in Settings. The full details are in the [privacy policy](https://yawnandpawn.com/privacy/).

## 7. When the test ends

After **[END DATE: owner fills in]** you can keep using the app or uninstall it. To leave the test, open the opt-in link again and tap "Leave the program". Please don't leave before the end date: if fewer than 12 testers stay for 14 days in a row, the 14 days start again.

Thank you. We hope you never need to snooze.
