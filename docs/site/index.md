# Yawn & Pawn

Yawn & Pawn is an Android alarm clock where every snooze costs real money, and each snooze in the same morning costs more than the last. Waking up is always free: you stop the alarm by finishing a check you chose, such as a few math problems or scanning a barcode in another room.

The app makes money only when you snooze. We hope you never pay us.
