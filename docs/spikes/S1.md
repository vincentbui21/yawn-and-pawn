# Spike S1: pay for a snooze over the lock screen

**Story:** 1.5 · **Refs:** FR-RNG-3, FR-RNG-5, AD-2, AD-7, OQ-1; PRD §10 S1 · **Verify:** human-verify (the owner runs it; automation never marks the story done)

**Prototype:** branch `spike/s1-billing-lockscreen` only (never merged to `main`), release 101 / `0.1.1-spike` on the Internal testing track. Code: `androidApp/src/main/kotlin/com/yawnandpawn/app/android/spike/`. Owner steps: [S1-owner-steps.md](S1-owner-steps.md).

**Test product:** `spike_s1_test`, a one-time product at the lowest USD price, used as a consumable (the app consumes it), pending purchases enabled. Created by hand for the spike only and **deactivated after it** (Story 4.1's `tools/play-catalog` reports it as "unmanaged").

## Deviation from the story

The story asks for at least a Pixel and a Samsung device, 5 runs each. **Only the owner's Oppo A96 (ColorOS, Android 13) is available**, so every run below is on that one phone. ColorOS changes the lock screen and background rules, so a result here may differ on Pixel (stock Android) and Samsung (One UI). Any decision that depends on a lock-screen detail is marked "Oppo only" and gets re-checked on Pixel and Samsung before Epic 4 ships (to `deferred-work.md`).

## How the prototype gathers evidence

- `SpikeS1Activity` shows over the lock screen (`setShowWhenLocked` / `setTurnScreenOn`), plays `alarm_default.ogg` looping on `USAGE_ALARM` with `STREAM_ALARM` at maximum, and shows the lock state (`KeyguardManager.isKeyguardLocked`), billing connection, product details, last result and Pay → result timings.
- **Pay**: when locked, `requestDismissKeyguard` (logs `onDismissSucceeded` / `onDismissCancelled` / `onDismissError`), then `launchBillingFlow` with `obfuscatedProfileId` = a new random UUID and `obfuscatedAccountId` = one random id per install. **Pay without unlock**: `launchBillingFlow` straight away, even while locked.
- Every step and billing callback is logged under tag `SpikeS1` with `t=<elapsedRealtime>` and `+<ms since Pay>`: `adb logcat -v time -s SpikeS1`, or the **Share log** button. A `RESULT pay=#n/... payToResultMs=...` line closes each Pay. While a purchase is in flight, a `sound monitor` line every 2 s gives `playing` and the alarm-stream volume.
- Purchase tokens are logged by their last 6 characters only.

Open it from the **Spike S1** launcher icon (own task, next to Yawn & Pawn), with `adb shell am start -a android.intent.action.VIEW -d yawnandpawn://spike-s1`, or from the "Ring in 15 s" full-screen notification (an exact `setAlarmClock` alarm, so the phone can be locked first). It is in debug and release builds on the spike branch.

Branch-only build changes: Play Billing `com.android.billingclient:billing-ktx:9.1.0` (Stack table version) on `:androidApp`, its five runtime coordinates in `config/dependency-allowlist.txt`, and versionCode 101 / versionName `0.1.1-spike` hard-set in `androidApp/build.gradle.kts`. On this branch `qualityGate` fails only in `AppVersioningTest` (it parses the versionName as `major.minor.patch`, and `0.1.1-spike` is not); that check is left as it is on `main`.

Known limits of the prototype: the sound plays from the app process, not from a foreground service as the real `WakeService` does. Payment sheets may show black in screen recordings (secure surface), so for locked runs a second phone filming the screen is better evidence than the built-in recorder.

**Other findings**
- **EU consent before every purchase:** this Finnish account sees Play's "Review and agree" (right of withdrawal) screen before **every** purchase, not just the first. That adds one tap per paid snooze.
- **First purchase on the account** also asks "Require authentication for purchases?" (one time; answered "No, thanks").
- **A declined card** has no distinct code: Play shows "Declined…" with "Got it", then `BILLING_UNAVAILABLE(3)` (C1u).
- **Consume can fail transiently** with `SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE(2)` "Server error, please try again" (after L5); a retry 16 s later succeeded. The real adapter needs a consume retry.
- `ITEM_ALREADY_OWNED(7)` when paying again before consuming.
- **Anomaly to re-check:** a slow-card purchase (V1s, PURCHASED 20:06:14, not consumed) was gone from `queryPurchasesAsync` by 20:11:36. The C2u slow-card purchase was still owned 48 s after it completed.

## Decision

_Proposed by Claude from the findings; **B chosen by the owner on 2026-10-05**._

### Unlock mechanics for FR-RNG-3

- **A (no unlock)** is ruled out: the Play sheet never shows over a keyguard (question 1).
- **Proposed: B. Unlock first, inside the billing adapter.** The confirm sheet shows the price over the lock screen. "Pay" calls `requestDismissKeyguard`, the PIN prompt appears, and `onDismissSucceeded` launches billing. `onDismissCancelled` returns to the ringing screen with the sound on. The user still sees the price before unlocking, and it matched the runs: L1–L5 succeeded and V2 recovered cleanly.
- C (unlock before the confirm sheet) also works, but it hides the price until after unlocking and adds a step before the user knows what they're paying.

Decision: **B. Price first, then PIN** (owner, 2026-10-05). The confirm sheet shows the price over the lock screen; Pay asks for the PIN; Play's sheet opens after `onDismissSucceeded`; cancelling the PIN returns to the ringing alarm.

Does AD-2 need `UnlockRequested` / `UnlockFailed` events? **Yes.** Use an "unlocking" sub-state while the PIN prompt is up. Cancel (`onDismissCancelled`, or `onDismissError` if it ever fires) returns to Ringing with no billing. A wrong PIN produces no event, so the state waits; the existing 30-minute forgotten-alarm stop remains the backstop.

### New AD-2 transition rows (to apply through `bmad-correct-course` before Epic 4)

| From | Event | Guard | To | One-shot effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ringing (confirm sheet open) | PayConfirmed | keyguard locked | Unlocking | RequestKeyguardDismiss |
| Ringing (confirm sheet open) | PayConfirmed | keyguard not locked | Paying | LaunchBilling(profileId) |
| Unlocking | UnlockSucceeded | | Paying | LaunchBilling(profileId) |
| Unlocking | UnlockFailed (cancelled or error) | | Ringing | none (sound never stopped) |

### Contradictions to raise

These go through `bmad-correct-course` before Epic 4:

- **The sheet cannot be shown while locked** (V1s). Any spine or PRD text that assumes payment over the lock screen needs the unlock step.
- **The volume keys can turn the alarm stream down while the Play sheet is open** (U1, L1). Options: the wake runtime re-asserts the alarm-stream volume while a purchase is in flight, or the PRD accepts it.
- **There's no timeout on the Play sheet offline** (N1u). The adapter must not block the session on a billing result. The alarm keeps ringing, and the session needs its own timeout or cancel path.
- **Consume can fail transiently** (L5). The adapter needs a retry, and must handle `ITEM_ALREADY_OWNED` by consuming the leftover.
- **The EU consent screen appears every time** (all runs). This adds one tap to each paid snooze; account for it in the UX copy and timing.
